I'm pursuing an **M.S. in Data Science at the University of Minnesota Twin Cities**, graduating in **May 2027**. I also work as a Research Assistant in the School of Public Health, studying digital health engagement using national survey data.

My work spans retrieval-augmented generation, multimodal recommendation, computer vision, and statistical modeling. I enjoy connecting model development with useful software: preparing data, building APIs and interfaces, and evaluating how well the system works.

Previously, as an AI Fellow at OViIE, I worked on STELLA, a multilingual voice translation system. I earned my B.Tech in Artificial Intelligence and Data Science from Sri Venkateswara College of Engineering, Anna University.
